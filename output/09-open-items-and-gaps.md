## 2a. MAX31856 incoming inspection (written in the Cowork session 2026-09-25, landed on disk 2026-09-26)
The generic MAX31856 2-pack (Amazon, received by 2026-10-01) is CRITICAL PATH: both MAX31855 boards were defective and returned (M8), so there is no interim pit sensor. Test each board in this order before wiring it to the bench:
1. Chip laser-marked MAX31856 with the Maxim or ADI logo; board layout matching the Adafruit 3263.
2. 3.3 V on 3Vo with 5 V in (the board regulator), before the S3 sees it.
3. Register readback, the decisive test: setThermocoupleType(J), getThermocoupleType() returns J, then set K. A MAX31855 has no writable registers, so a relabeled part cannot pass. Library calls VERIFIED in Adafruit_MAX31856.h lines 121 and 122 (v1.2.8, installed in .pio\libdeps).
4. Cold junction reads room temperature with nothing attached.
5. readFault() raises the open-circuit bit with the thermocouple disconnected; clears with T+ shorted to T-.
6. Shorted-input check: thermocouple reading equals the cold junction within about 2 C (the test that failed the MAX31855s).
7. Ice and boiling water, then console `o <C>` for the offset.
If a board fails: buy the Adafruit 3263, not another unbranded board. There is no MAX31855 fallback any more.

## 6a. New firmware work created by the blower decision (2026-09-24)
The Delta is a 4-wire brushless fan, so the drive model changes. BUILT 2026-10-04, build-verified and unit-tested, NOT run against a fan (detail and results in doc 05, "4-wire fan support").
- BUILT: PWM polarity inverts (open-drain driver). `cfg::BLOWER_4WIRE` selects it; `-DBLOWER_LEGACY_2WIRE` builds the old model.
- BUILT: PWM frequency held to 21 to 28 kHz, default 25 kHz (Intel 4-wire range), in the console `f` and at NVS load. The 25 kHz vs 200 Hz bench comparison in doc 02 no longer applies once the Delta is fitted.
- OPEN, bench: datasheet minimum start duty is at most 30 percent, so the long-PWM path stays in use. Measure the real start duty and the lower RUN duty (M5), now readable as fan_rpm in the CSV.
- BUILT: tach input on a PCNT pulse counter with a 10 us glitch filter, RPM in the `d` dump and the CSV. OPEN, bench: the datasheet FG waveform shows four equal intervals per revolution on a 4-pole motor, which reads as 2 pulses per revolution; NOT confirmed by measurement, so count it and set it with `p <n>` before trusting an RPM figure.
- BUILT: fault BLOWER_STALL: duty above 0 with no tach pulses for 3 s. As built, the 3 s are driven seconds: long-PWM off time neither counts nor clears. An open thermostat contact or a dead 12 V supply also shows up as this fault.
- BUILT: fault BLOWER_RUNAWAY: measured RPM far above the commanded speed for 5 s, which is what a broken PWM lead looks like on this fan. Action: drop SUPPLY_EN (cuts the fan through the IRLZ44N) and fault. As built, "far above" is RPM greater than commanded percent of 4400 plus 25 percent of 4400 (1100 RPM); at the 60 percent duty cap that is 3740 RPM, under the 3960 RPM of a fan at the low end of the +/- 10 percent tolerance. OPEN, bench: the margin assumes speed roughly linear in duty; check it against measured RPM at 30 to 60 percent, a fan that runs well above linear would fault falsely.
- BUILT: the IRLZ44N moves from carrying PWM to a supply cut on its own GPIO. As built it is cut in IDLE as well as in FAULT, and on at every other time, including duty 0 (the fan is held stopped through the PWM lead, and the runaway check watches it).
- DONE earlier: pins.h has TACH and SUPPLY_EN for all three board maps (doc 04).
- CLOSED 2026-10-10 (doc 05 "S3 fan bench"): with the fan running at 30 percent, BLUE opened gave BLOWER_RUNAWAY 5 s after the RPM crossed the threshold, and YELLOW pulled gave BLOWER_STALL after 3 driven seconds; both cut the supply and the fan stopped. ppr 2 confirmed (4615 RPM at 100 percent). The 4-wire firmware is validated on the Delta. Still open from this list: M5 running current, and `n` (min start) to be lowered from 30 once the 10 percent start is repeated.
- Bench switch: `T 0` turns both tach faults off until the next boot, for a board with no fan attached.
